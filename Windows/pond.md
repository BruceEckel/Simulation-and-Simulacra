# pond

A pond at dusk that answers the pointer slowly and asks nothing:
rings, a wake, and a lantern that walks when you leave it alone.

## The idea

A dark surface of water.
Move the pointer and a small light comes after it, a moment behind, and leaves a wake.
Press the button and the water dips under it;
let go and the dip rebounds into a ring that spreads out and fades.
Hold the button longer and the dip grows while you hold it, so the ring is bigger when it comes.
Drag while holding and the dip follows you as a furrow.
Leave the pointer alone for ten seconds and the light goes for a slow walk of its own, side to side across the water, at a pace your eyes can follow without trying.
Move the pointer and it comes back to you.

That is all of it.
There is no score, no timer, no way to lose, and nothing that needs a quick hand.
Rings pass through rings.
Every one of them dies away on its own, the edges of the pond absorb rather than reflect, and given a minute the water is flat again.
Now and then a drop falls somewhere, so it is not quite still and so you can see what a press does without being told.

The walk borrows one idea from EMDR:
a light crossing the visual field from one side to the other and back, over and over, with nothing asked of you but to watch it.
It is not a treatment;
a treatment involves a person trained to give one.
It is a place to rest your eyes.
The pace starts slower than a clinician would set it and you can make it slower still.

The water is a real simulation rather than a drawing of one.
The surface is the wave equation run on a grid of a quarter of a million cells, and everything you see is that surface, lit from above and looked through to a bed of colour underneath.
A ring is a crest with a trough behind it, and it takes its colour from how high the water is:
crests from the bright end of the palette, troughs from the dark end.

## Working it

Everything worth doing is done with the mouse, and you can find all of it by playing.

| | |
|---|---|
| move | the lantern follows |
| press, then let go | a ring; hold longer for a bigger one |
| press and drag | a furrow |
| scroll | the colours drift |
| leave it alone | the lantern walks |

The pointer is hidden over the water.
The lantern is where the pointer is, a moment behind.

| | |
|---|---|
| `c` | the next palette: dusk, lagoon, ember, moon, meadow, aurora |
| `up` / `down` | hold for a quicker or a slower walk |
| `space` | send the lantern walking now, or call it back |
| `r` | still the water |
| `h` | the hint, on or off; it goes away on its own after a while |
| `F11` | the whole display, and back to a window |

It opens in an ordinary window.
`F11` takes the display with no border, and `F11` again gives the window back.
